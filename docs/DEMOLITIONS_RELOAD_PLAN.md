# Demolitions and hinged reload implementation plan

Status: installed-asset and SDK audit, 2026-10-01. No launcher adapter, server change, game build, game input, or headset acceptance is claimed by this document. The next scope is the M32 six-chamber launcher plus a shared break-action foundation for M79 and BoomStick. The owner explicitly requests individual removal/replacement and physical flick closure. Closing is a mechanical operation; it must never reset or refill ammunition.

## M32: native side hinge, distinct cylinder indexing

The joint opening sideways is **`Upper_Reciever` relative to `Lower_Receiver`**, not `Magazine Cylinder`. It rotates 51 degrees about the chassis longitudinal axis: closed through Reload frame 5, opening frames 6–8, held open through 288, then closing 289–291. Frame 290 is still about 50 degrees open and frame 291 is closed. Native closure is abrupt; scrub or interpolate the measured joint endpoints with a bounded visual close transition. Do not replay all 300 frames, move the tracked lower receiver, or let interpolation complete any ammo transaction.

The cylinder rotates separately by approximately 60 degrees relative to `Upper_Reciever`. Its stock animation repeatedly returns to a common pose between loading cycles (for example frame 45 → 46 resets, then frames 49–52 turn again). This is an authored reuse trick, not persistent six-chamber indexing. A VR chamber-index variable must survive opening, closing, tracking recovery and weapon changes; a presentation reset cannot become an ammo reset.

The five dummy shell meshes remain stationary **in cylinder space throughout Reload**. Each has 49 points principally weighted to its bone, approximately 44 mm long. `HE_Shell` has 56 such points and the same approximate casing length; `HE_Round` has 96 points and is approximately 81 mm long. The source repeatedly animates one casing and one fresh grenade, rather than six independently authored shell transfers. It supports the user's individual extraction policy; it is not evidence of a collective eject-all requirement.

`HE_Round` is parked away from the chamber in Idle. Its Idle root cannot be reused as a magazine-style seated socket. The live prop seats at the `HE_Shell` chamber location. Dummy-shell and HE-shell roots also have different axial offsets although their casing surfaces align. Derive a chamber contact/surface basis from the installed geometry and seated sample, then measure each socket relative to the cylinder; do not place a full live prop at a dummy root without its bind-offset conversion.

For scalable presentation, keep the original body/receiver/cylinder and hide/pose chamber components from the ledger. A local installer/converter may derive a reusable live-round/casing visual from the existing weighted geometry, then instantiate that part at six measured socket transforms or in the offhand. It must preserve source materials and original weapon hand/support/muzzle transforms. No proprietary coordinates or extracted mesh enters tracked code. Closed casing-only props do not by themselves prove an adequate open-cylinder view of all live grenade noses; geometry/render inspection remains required.

## M79 and BoomStick: shared break action, different chamber assets

M79 `Barrel` opens from Fire frame 11 through 16 to 34 degrees, holds through 80, overshoots to 46 degrees at 84 and closes by 86. A conservative native open reference is .16; close references are .84 → .86. The local joint rotates about a different axis from the M32 side hinge. The native 34 → 46 degree anticipation need not be interpreted as an extra required wrist motion; the deliberate user flip recognizes closure intent, then the visual may use the measured close segment.

M79 `Empty_Shell` starts seated; extraction begins around frame 25. Its later very large offscreen translation is an animation hiding technique, not a ballistic casing trajectory. `Shell` is the fresh prop, parked in Idle. A useful final approach is Fire frames 55 → 65 (.55 → .65), about 34.5 mm; frames 62 → 65 cover the last 27.5 mm. A native left-hand grasp candidate at frame 38 (.38) has about 88 mm palm/root distance with stable sampled neighbouring joint relationships. The live prop is seated 65–86, then swaps away as the other prop returns. Calibrate the chamber from seated geometry; never use parked `Shell` Idle or confuse that handoff with a second cartridge.

BoomStick `Barrel` opens during Fire_Both frames 14–18 to 60 degrees, remains open through 78, and closes during 78–82. Proposed normalized hinge references are .20 and .86667 → .91111. Both stock shell props eject around frames 21–23; replacement is sequential: right-shell final approach 45–50 (about 42.7 mm), then left-shell 64–66 (about 42.1 mm). A native first-shell left grasp candidate is frame 32 / .35556, about 120 mm palm/root distance. Validate the second-shell handoff separately.

Because BoomStick shells are not Barrel children, changing only the hinge leaves retained rounds floating in the closed position. Derive each seated shell transform relative to `Barrel` and apply it through the actual parent when the breech moves. Additionally, `Shell_right` and `Shell_left` occupy swapped lateral positions at the start of Fire_Both versus Idle. Name strings alone cannot identify a persistent physical chamber: assign socket IDs from measured closed geometry and the two actual muzzle tips, and keep those IDs stable through every pose.

The shared component should own the hinge's `Closed`, `Opening`, `Open`, `Closing` lifecycle and chamber-independent close gesture. M79 supplies one socket; BoomStick supplies two. Each independently stores live/spent/empty state and typed round ownership. For the first prototype, manual removal is explicit per chamber and retains unfired rounds. The stock BoomStick collective eject pose may later present an explicit simultaneous spent-only eject event; it must not silently discard retained live shells or refill both chambers. This separates observed collective animation from the selected manual interaction policy.

## Chamber ledger and conservation contract

Proposed isolated component: `KFVRChamberState` Object plus commandlet tests, with fixed maximum six chambers and configured active count 1, 2 or 6. This is a new component, not a magazine with its capacity set to six. Each chamber has `Empty`, `Live`, or `Spent` and an identity; one offhand-owned item is either a live round or a spent casing. Supply and returning storage are typed to the weapon's actual fire-mode ammunition class and ownership epoch. M32, M79 and hand grenades are not interchangeable merely because all are called grenades. The installed SDK contains distinct `M32Ammo`, `M79Ammo`, `M203Ammo`, `LAWAmmo` classes; verify each adapter's actual FireMode AmmoClass before enabling supply.

| Accepted operation | Preconditions | Exact state effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open | Valid held weapon; latch interaction; no active shot or conflicting hand ownership | Changes mechanism only. |
| Extract chamber i | Open; measured grip/casing contact and withdrawal; stable identity; free offhand | One Live/Spent chamber becomes Empty and transfers that exact item to the hand. No reserve credit. |
| Return live round | Held live item reaches its compatible chest storage with deliberate release | Transfers that one owned item into reserve storage; no new round. |
| Discard | Explicit release away from storage/insertion; original identity present | A live item increments discarded-live once; a spent casing is cosmetic waste with no ammo credit. |
| Take chest round | Fresh grip edge, valid source, free offhand, reserve available | Reserve decreases by one, HeldLive becomes that round. No whole-cylinder batch. |
| Seat chamber i | Open; Empty target; matching held live identity; continuous aligned insertion | HeldLive → Live[i] exactly once. No timer-created rounds and no implicit index change. |
| Index M32 | Open manual cylinder interaction or a separately admitted closed firing/dry-index event | Changes selected chamber/detent only; never shuffles or refills identities. |
| Close / flick close | Open; nothing intersecting/inserting/grasping mechanism; measured complete angular stroke; any loaded count | Locks mechanism and preserves every chamber, reserve, held item and counter. Empty, partial and full cylinders may close. |
| Fire | Closed/latched; selected Live chamber(s); stock authority, fire cooldown and native safety conditions | One accepted consumption maps each fired Live → Spent; native ammo decreases by exactly the same count. |

Invariant: `initial live budget + legitimate acquired ammo = reserve live + held live + retained live storage + live chambers + spent-shot count + discarded-live count`. Spent cases are not usable ammunition and must not be counted a second time in that equation. Use a separate casing identity/counter for visuals. Native `AmmoAmount` includes loaded rounds; changing location from pouch to hand to chamber does not subtract native total ammo again. Mirror `MagAmmoRemaining` from Live chamber count for stock HUD/interop; that count cannot decide which particular chamber can fire.

Tactical reload preserves unfired chamber identities. A grip on Live removes a retained round, not a spent case. An occupied chamber rejects insertion without mutation. Repeated packet/edge/seat callbacks cannot duplicate an item. Tracking loss cancels an unfinished gesture without crediting an extraction, insertion or close; committed held ownership remains recoverable and requires release/regrab. Switching/parking/death must clean pose/hand ownership and persist or explicitly reconcile the ledger without a default full cylinder. A stock weapon imported mid-session has only a loaded count, not spent-case history: use a documented deterministic mapping and treat other chambers as Empty, never invent spent history or ammunition.

For M32, track a real current chamber and a visual detent separately from the fixed socket IDs. A closed trigger on an empty/spent chamber needs an explicit dry-index policy using a fresh trigger edge and stock-compatible cadence; do not automatically skip to any live chamber. Whether the first prototype permits direct grabbing of all six exposed sockets or uses an explicit cylinder index gesture is an interaction choice, not an excuse to reload all six. Start with individually addressed visible sockets and add index manipulation only once contact geometry is verified.

## Physical flick closure and time domains

The user requested **sideways wrist flick closure for M32** and **flip closure for M79/BoomStick**. Implement one shared angular-stroke detector with profile-specific measured hinge basis, closure sign and allowed direction; do not use hardcoded world left/right or share the M32 axis with the break barrel. Gesture thresholds are authored interaction parameters requiring synthetic and wearer testing, not values inferred from animation duration.

Consume only fresh, clock-valid `InputSerial` / `InputSeconds` samples. Use the mechanic controller orientation before visual recoil, support alignment or hand snapping, expressed relative to the current body frame and then the calibrated weapon/grip basis. Never derive closure from `KFVRWeaponVisual.Rotation`: recoil settling alone could otherwise close the mechanism. The existing presentation controller orientation is aim-oriented, not raw grip orientation; preserve that accepted contract and explicitly transform the profile basis. If raw grip rotation is added later, give it a separate field instead of reinterpreting current fields.

An open mechanism arms only after a neutral, tracked pose and any conflicting grip is released. Accumulate a bounded signed angular excursion along the closing axis, require a minimum angular speed and complete travel, reject excessive off-axis rotation, then latch one close event. Walking is translation and cannot close it. Ordinary slow turning, snap-turn/body-reference changes, recenter, focus loss, holster/switch, owner or weapon change, sample rollback/gap, and either required hand losing tracking cancel the candidate and require neutral/rearm. Freeze/reset the gesture on reference discontinuity even if the new pose looks like a successful endpoint. Do not accumulate tiny oscillations over an arbitrarily old origin; use bounded stroke age and aggregate speed as well as instantaneous speed. Close must not trigger merely because grip is released, a timeout elapsed, or tracking resumed near closed pose.

Physical detection uses source time, unaffected by ZED time. Stock firing cooldown and deliberate reload balance use game time and the current perk policy. Closing presentation can interpolate a native measured joint after a recognized event, but interpolation has no ammunition authority. A later optional two-hand guided close should share this exact ledger close operation; it is not a replacement for the requested flick.

## Stock timing, projectile authority and adapter boundaries

Installed `KFWeapon.ReloadMeNow` computes `ReloadRate = Default.ReloadRate / GetReloadSpeedModifier`. `KFWeapon.WeaponTick` calls `AddReloadedAmmo` after that game-time interval, repeats for hold-to-reload weapons and otherwise fills their magazine count. `KFWeaponShotgun.AddReloadedAmmo` increments one loaded round and emits the stock reload achievement callback. M32 inherits this path: the measured class default is capacity 6, `bHoldToReload=True`, `ReloadRate≈1.634`. The ten-second clip and approximately six per-round intervals agree in scale, but are different sources of authority.

A physical M32 adapter must suppress timed AddReloadedAmmo and all automatic loaded-count writes while it owns the ledger. Preserve deliberate stock pacing with a per-round game-time readiness deadline using the same perk modifier: completion requires both valid physical removal/insertion and the gate. If insertion is too early, remain an owned, unfinished insertion requiring continued valid contact; a timer alone must never seat a round after the hand leaves. Closing early never bypasses outstanding fire/readiness deadlines. Exact gating allocation should be recorded and measured against stock empty and tactical reloads before activation.

M79's measured `ReloadRate=.01` and `ReloadAnimRate=0` do **not** establish a .01-second reload. `M79Fire.AllowFire` checks total ammo, and its long Fire animation contains the reload; inherited fire-mode `NextFireTime` / `FireRate` supplies essential cadence. Read actual inherited fire defaults before implementing its timing gate. LAW likewise has a combined AimFire sequence; `LAWFire.AllowFire` requires rifle aiming and completed zoom, and firing calls ZoomOut. A VR adapter must deliberately bridge that aim gate while retaining all unrelated stock safety/cooldown checks. LAW is not a reusable break-action profile.

BoomStick has additional bypasses: `WeaponTick` refills to two after `CurrentReloadCountDown`; `AmmoPickedUp` and `GiveTo` can refill when empty; `SetPendingReload` arms the countdown; `SingleShotCount` and delayed replication independently drive firing. `BoomStickFire` is the dual-shot mode and sets that pending reload. `BoomStickAltFire` consumes one, modifies SingleShotCount, invokes the projectile effect and uses `FireLastRate` after the last chamber. Gate all those refill paths and mirror the ledger consistently in both firing modes, including the stock one-round alternate fallback. Leaving the generic KFWeapon timer disabled is insufficient.

Keep stock-derived fire classes, projectile selection, `SpawnProjectile`/`ForceSpawnProjectile`, instigator, `PostSpawnProjectile` and DamageAtten, firing cadence, spread/perk hooks and obstruction protection. `Engine.WeaponFire.ModeDoFire` performs ammo consumption and effects on ROLE_Authority; the VR adapter changes the admitted muzzle origin/direction and chamber gate. Do not replace launcher shots with hitscan or hand-grenade throws. Since the inherited method calls DoFireEffect after ConsumeAmmo, a rejected consumption must also prevent the associated effect through the same admitted shot token; no projectile may spawn after a ledger rejection, and no round may be consumed twice.

`M32GrenadeProjectile` inherits `M79GrenadeProjectile`, whose stock arming-distance/dud, ballistic, explosion, siren-disintegration and damage logic must remain authoritative. `KFVetDemolitions.AddDamage` recognizes launcher damage types; creating a generic replacement projectile risks breaking that policy. Nothing here enables network play: standalone guards remain until the separate network design is implemented and tested.

## Reuse limits and next bounded prototype

`Magnum44Pistol` and `FlareRevolver` are candidates for the six-chamber identity component, but currently implement stock bulk AddReloadedAmmo (and the Magnum reload achievement). Their own cylinder assets, capacity, socket mapping, speedloader/ejection policy and dual versions need separate audits. Do not inherit M32 geometry or physical closing direction. `SPGrenadeLauncher` uses a similar stock fire abstraction to M79 but its asset was not audited here. `M4203AssaultRifle` has independent secondary `M203Fire` ammo/cooldown/zoom/reload guards; its rifle magazine and launcher chamber need separate ledgers. SeekerSix and SealSqueal are distinct unaudited launcher mechanisms, not automatic cylinder coverage.

Next implementation should be isolated ledger and angular-gesture Objects plus commandlets, then one hidden native sampling/presentation fixture for the M32 and one for M79/BoomStick. Proposed boundaries are `KFVRChamberState`, `KFVRHingeGesture`, mechanism-specific profiles and stock-derived adapters. These names are design proposals, not current files. Reuse current source-clock validity, ownership reservation, chest-location query and reversible native bone calibration; do not append this state to the single-pistol magazine ledger.

Required acceptance cases before broad enabling:

1. For 1/2/6 chambers, all Live/Spent/Empty combinations, tactical retention, absent supply, occupied-slot rejection, repeated input and interleaved switch/ownership changes conserve every live identity. Closing from every loaded count changes no ammunition; randomized extraction/return/discard/fire sequences preserve the budget.
2. Each legitimate angular stroke closes once under normal, slow and ramping game time. Duplicate samples, wrong direction, incomplete travel, old accumulated oscillation, walking, snap turn, recoil, recenter, hand/focus loss and held recovery cannot close it. Reference/owner changes never resume an old stroke.
3. With no physical actions, wait longer than every stock reload/fire timer and pick up ammo: no chamber loads itself. With physical actions completed before balance gates, no premature shot or unattended timer seat occurs. Verify perk changes and cooldowns in game time.
4. Read actual native joint and shell transforms independently: stable chassis/accepted grip/muzzle, correct M32 side hinge and persistent index, retained BoomStick shells following its hinge, M79 prop handoff, and no shell left in an offscreen animation hiding pose. Reject missing bones and same-frame cache evidence.
5. In one bounded private live fixture, each admitted trigger maps to exactly the expected stock ammo decrement and projectile(s); open/dry/cooldown/ledger rejection maps to none. Near-wall shots preserve stock spawn obstruction safety. Actual projectile class and damage type remain stock-derived.

This audit makes a concrete three-weapon mechanism milestone possible. It does not complete Demolitions, solve all launchers, prove multiplayer compatibility, or replace the eventual headset ergonomics test.
